About me

I'm a Computer Science student at VIT Chennai, specialising in Cyber Physical Systems, and I'm happiest where software meets hardware. Robotics, AI and machine learning are what I keep coming back to.

My two favourite builds show why. The Smart Walker uses ultrasonic, IMU and load cell sensors to warn an elderly user about obstacles and possible falls. The Autonomous Rover maps its surroundings with LiDAR SLAM and spots fire, smoke and people with YOLO, and I'm now adding Reinforcement Learning for navigation.

I also apply ML to healthcare images, from coronary artery segmentation (paper submitted) to dental malocclusion detection with Vision Transformers. As a student coordinator and Rotary Club environmental lead, I enjoy leading teams and designing posters, too.
